Delete your account and your data

Last updated: September 12, 2026 · Contact: kuskalabs@gmail.com

Quipu has no servers or accounts of its own: you sign in with your Google account and your data lives in your private Google Sheet and on your device. So "deleting your Quipu account" means disconnecting the app and deleting that data. You have two ways:

Option A — From the app (immediate)

  1. Open Quipu and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Sign out / Disconnect Google. This deletes the token, PIN and local cache from your device instantly.
  3. (Optional) In your Google Drive, trash the INCOME AND EXPENSES spreadsheet to delete your transactions.
  4. (Optional) Revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Option B — By email (we help you)

  1. Write to kuskalabs@gmail.com with subject "Delete my data — Quipu" from your Google account email.
  2. We confirm by email when done, within a maximum of 30 days.

What data is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: OAuth token, Sheet ID, PIN, local history, tag catalogue, transaction cache and preferences on your device (on disconnect/uninstall). Your Sheet rows, when you delete the spreadsheet in Drive.
  • Kept: nothing on our servers, because we have none. Google keeps your data under its own policies until you delete it in Drive.
  • Extra retention: 0 days on our own infrastructure. Support emails are deleted after 90 days.
