2. What Quipu does

Quipu is a React Native/Expo app with no backend that uses your private Google Sheet as its database (tabs INCOME AND EXPENSES and MONTHLY SUMMARY). Your descriptions stay exactly as you type them; calculations (totals, summaries) are informational and depend on your Sheet.

3. Google account and your Sheet

  • You need a Google account. Initial login requests no Drive/Sheets scopes; the drive.metadata.readonly and spreadsheets scopes are requested only when you connect your sheet (incremental).
  • You are responsible for managing your Sheet (permissions, trash, backups). If you trash it, the app clears its local cache and creates a new one if no compatible sheet is found.
  • You can disconnect/revoke at any time from Settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
