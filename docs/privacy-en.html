1. Summary

Quipu helps you track income and expenses. We have no servers of our own. Your information lives in your private Google Sheet in your Drive and in your phone's encrypted local storage (SecureStore for token/PIN). We do not sell or share your data.

2. What data we collect

  • Google account: name and email provided by Google Sign-In (only to display your account and link your Sheet).
  • OAuth token and Sheet ID: stored locally with SecureStore and in the local cache to work offline.
  • Transactions you type: date, amount, detail, type (FREQUENT/NON-FREQUENT INCOME/EXPENSE), tags and creation time. They stay in your Sheet.
  • Cosmetic preferences: language, currency symbol, font and palette (synced to MONTHLY SUMMARY!L1:L2 if you enable Sheets).
  • Local PIN (optional): if you enable a PIN, it only lives encrypted on your device, never leaves it.

We do not collect location, contacts, or third-party data.

3. What we use it for

  • Authenticating you and connecting your Drive/Sheets.
  • Finding/creating your INCOME AND EXPENSES spreadsheet and reading/writing your movements.
  • Showing your offline balance from cache and reconciling on reconnect.

4. Google scopes (incremental)

We only request these two after you pick an account (initial login requests no scopes):

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.metadata.readonly — list/find your Sheet in Drive (metadata only, never reads arbitrary files).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets — read/write your finance Sheet.

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. Where your data lives

  • Google Drive / Sheets: your INCOME AND EXPENSES spreadsheet (tabs INCOME AND EXPENSES and MONTHLY SUMMARY) is your database.
  • Device: local JSON cache and SecureStore (token, Sheet ID, PIN, 30-day history and tag catalogue). The app paints cache first and revalidates in the background.

If you trash your Sheet in Drive, the app clears its cache and starts fresh.

6. Sharing with third parties

We share nothing. Only Google as authentication and storage provider (under their policies). No third-party analytics or ads.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Your transactions live as long as you keep your Sheet.
  • You can delete everything from the app (Disconnect Google) or by deleting the Sheet in Drive and clearing app data. The PIN is deleted when disabled.
  • Local history is purged after 30 days.

8. Security

  • Incremental OAuth with GoogleSignin.addScopes() only before reading/writing Drive/Sheets.
  • Tokens in SecureStore (keychain/keystore). Traffic encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Mutations with a serialized queue (syncQueue) and reconciliation so offline data is never lost.

9. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data by deleting rows in your Sheet or the whole sheet, and by revoking access. Write to kuskalabs@gmail.com and we will respond.

10. Children

App for ages 13 and up. Not directed at children.

11. Changes

We will publish changes here with a new date. Material changes will be announced on the Play listing.
